Furniture for Every Room

We create personalized furniture for every room in your home using your supplied materials. Our carpenters and machinery are at your service with three flexible pricing options:

  • Attendance-Based: Pay per carpenter per day.
  • Percentage-Based: Pay a fixed percentage of the material cost used.
  • Square Feet-Based: Pay based on the total area (sq. ft.) of the furniture.

You choose the plan, we deliver quality that fits your lifestyle and budget.
